My Dearest Reebah,

Happy Birthday, meri Reboo, meri Shahzadi, mera pyara sa Dadu 🐸❤️. Today isn’t just another date on the calendar — it’s the day Allah sent the sweetest, most precious gift into this world, and into my life.

Tumse mil kar meri zindagi sach mein badal si gayi. Tum woh wajah ho jiski wajah se main muskurata hoon, aur jiski wajah se maine apni zindagi mein itni sari achhi tabdeelian ki. Chahe main kitna bhi udaas ya thaka hua hoon, tumhari ek smile meri sari sadness khatam kar deti hai — you really are my medicine.

Aaj tumhare birthday par bas ek hi baat yaad rakhna: chahe jaisi bhi situation ho, agar tumhare sath koi bhi nahi hua na, toh yaad rakhna Ahmer phir bhi hoga. I promise to be the friend you truly deserve — standing beside you to celebrate every single success, holding you through every hard day, and always being there to wipe your tears.

Jitna mushkil waqt tumne dekha hai, meri dua hai Allah us se hazaar guna zyada khushiyan, sukoon aur kamyabi tumhare naseeb mein likh de. Tum bohat kamyab hona, dekhna tum — I believe in my Allah, and I believe in you, bachy.

No one can change my feelings for you, not even you. My little wish is that we never become strangers — because growing old with you is still my favorite dream.

Forever yours,
Ahmer❤️🐸🌙
